Music:
- incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod): a big catalogue of every genre. CC BY licence, so free with credit, or you can buy a no-credit licence.
- FreePD.com: public domain (CC0). No credit needed, and good for lobby and menu loops.
- Pixabay Music: its own licence, free for games with no credit required.
- OpenGameArt.org: made for games, with music and sound effects under mixed licences (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, GPL). Filter by licence.
- itch.io → Game Assets → Music: lots of free and cheap packs from indie composers, often game-ready loops.
- Unity Asset Store → Audio: covered by the Asset Store licence, so safe to use in a Unity game.

Sound effects (for trades, a station closing, UI clicks, the countdown beep):
- Kenney.nl: CC0, with clean UI and interface sound packs.
- Freesound.org: a huge library under mixed CC licences, so check each sound.
- Sonniss "GameAudioGDC" bundles: several gigabytes of professional, royalty-free sound effects released for free each year.
- ZapSplat: free with credit, or paid without.
